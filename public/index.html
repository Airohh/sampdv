MLOpsAI that maintains itself

Proths

ProblemWhen data shifts, a model degrades in silence, and a blind retrain can ship a worse one.

The fixA shift in the predicted-class mix (PSI) raises an alert and does not retrain. A drop in live accuracy does. Promotion needs +0.02 macro F1 on a fixed holdout.A change in the mix of articles raises an alert. It retrains only if live accuracy falls, and switches models only if the new one is clearly better.

Retraining is easy. Knowing it got better is the hard part.
